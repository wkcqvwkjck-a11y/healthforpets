Your pet's health record

Health for Pets brings together your pets' treatments, vaccines, parasite protection, reminders and health data — and shares them with your household.

Need help? Privacy policy

  • Treatments & reminders

    Dosages, daily doses, smart reminders and double-dose prevention so nothing is missed.

  • Health record

    Vaccines (WSAVA protocol), parasite protection, weight, allergies, documents and vet visits.

  • Condition tracking

    Diabetes, epilepsy, kidney disease, heart failure, mobility and dermatology.

  • Shared household

    With Pets+, several caregivers sync their pets' data in real time.

Questions?
